# AI Teammate: three-minute showcase plan

Production status: storyboard and shot list, not a recorded or submitted video. Use a captioned screen recording of the current local Meta terminal. Label simulated participants and synthetic evidence throughout. A later recording in a real group requires its own authorized platform session; Discord remains deferred.

## Story

A teammate returns after a discussion and needs to know the approved rule, what the evidence actually shows, and the next agreed check. Show one continuous chain from a new decision to an investigation brief and later recall. The payoff is a recoverable project decision and next step, with the source and approval still visible.

Working title: **AI Teammate — decisions that survive the conversation**.

Opening caption: **What did we agree, what do we know, and what happens next?**
